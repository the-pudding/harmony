How to record a synced song
  1. Pick a song and the cluster it belongs to. Its audio comes from static/assets/audio/synced-songs/{artist}__{song}.mp3 (or .mp4).
  2. Check the key. It starts as the corpus's key for the song, and every roman numeral is counted from it.
  3. Set the region, the part of the audio that has chord data. Drag the yellow handles on the waveform, or play to a spot and use start at playhead / end at playhead. The last chord lasts until the region ends.
  4. Connect a MIDI keyboard, press record, and play along: each chord you play is saved with its time, name and numeral. Play the full chord, or just its bass note to get the chord that belongs on that note in the key (F# in A major is F#m). Playing the same chord again records it again, so repeats show up. Recording replaces any chords the playhead passes over, so you can redo a passage.
  5. To fix a chord, drag it, or focus it and press ← → to nudge it (hold shift for bigger steps) or Delete to remove it. Space plays and pauses, and ⌘Z undoes.
  6. Save writes src/data/synced-songs/{song key}.json. This only works under npm run dev. Until you save, edits stay in this browser.
