Perfect vs. plagal cadences

How each section ends: a perfect (authentic) cadence resolves V→I, a plagal ("amen") cadence resolves IV→I. Both require landing on a genuine, unaltered tonic — not a borrowed chord. Decades need at least 20 dated, classifiable sections to appear.

Non-diatonic (borrowed) chords

Do these "eras" actually dominate their decade?

Three anecdotal chord-progression eras, checked against the corpus: blues (songs built on nothing but major I/IV/V), doo-wop (I-vi-IV-V), and the "four-chord song" axis-of-awesome family (I-V-vi-IV and its rotations). Each really does rise, peak, and fall roughly where the folk history says it should.
